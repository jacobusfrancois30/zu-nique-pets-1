    Permits, in plain terms

    South Africa protects its indigenous wildlife. Keeping, moving or selling an indigenous reptile, tortoise, chameleon or baboon spider needs a provincial nature conservation permit, and the rules differ between provinces. Several imported species carry CITES paperwork as well.

    Cards below carry a note where this applies. Ask us before you commit to an animal, and ask any seller to show you their paperwork. A seller who cannot is a seller to walk away from.
