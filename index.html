    Care advisory

    We would rather talk you out of it than sell you the wrong pet

    Most exotics that struggle are in the wrong setup rather than the wrong hands. Tell us about your room, your budget and your week, and we'll match an animal to it honestly, including when the answer is "not yet".

    Already have a pet that's struggling?

    Send a photo of the enclosure on WhatsApp. Heat, humidity and lighting problems usually show up in one picture, and they are usually cheap to fix.
